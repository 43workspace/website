### Events Space

Host workshops, trainings, small meetups or client sessions in our flexible events space. The room is equipped with seating, projector/TV, and high-speed internet. Centrally located near Kalyan Nagar (HRBR Layout) on the Outer Ring Road.

### Why book here

Hosting outside your own office gives an event a bit more occasion — a neutral, professional room for a workshop, meetup or client session, without the overhead of renting a full venue. It's an easy trip for attendees coming from most parts of the city.

### FAQ

<div class="faq" markdown="0">

<details>
<summary>What kind of events can I host?</summary>
<p>43 Events is suitable for tech meetups, non-profits events and corporate mixers.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the capacity of the space?</summary>
<p>Approx 1200 sqft. Can seat 40-50 people comfortably.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the amenities available?</summary>
<p>We have a large projection TV (85”), along with presentation A/V equipment and a stage area. Guest internet available. We can arrange for events to be live streamed.</p>
<p>The facility also has two washrooms. We can arrange for food &amp; beverage for an extra cost. There are limited facilities in the basement for dispensing. We expect you to clean up after yourself. Plenty of street parking in the vicinity.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How can I book the space?</summary>
<p>The space is available typically on weekends. Reservation is for 4 hours (typically) but available for full days. You can book the space using the 43 works app.</p>
</details>
