			<h2>Our Philosophy</h2>
		</header>

		<ul class="fact-list">
			<li>
				<strong>Affordable, no frills.</strong>
				No pantry, snack bar or coffee machine — go out for that.
			</li>
			<li>
				<strong>Walk to work.</strong>
				Ideally, you're close enough to skip the commute entirely.
			</li>
			<li>
				<strong>Clean & reliable.</strong>
				We keep it that way, every day.
			</li>
			<li>
				<strong>Tech-friendly, self-service.</strong>
				Built for people who'd rather get on with it than wait on someone.
			</li>
		</ul>
