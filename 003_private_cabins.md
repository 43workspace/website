### Private Cabins

Move in and start working — private cabins come fully furnished with high-speed internet, power backup and lift access, plus monthly meeting room credits. Available in 2 to 7 seater configurations, so you can start small and grow without changing addresses.

### Why a private cabin

A private cabin gives your team a quiet, lockable space of its own — without signing a long lease or managing an office yourself. Everything is set up before you walk in: furniture, internet, power backup. And because cabins come in sizes from 2 to 7 seats, you can grow your team without having to move your registered address again.
