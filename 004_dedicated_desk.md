### Dedicated Desk

A dedicated desk is reserved just for you — the same desk, every day, in our open workspace. If you want a consistent spot to work from without the cost or commitment of a private cabin, this is it.

### Why a dedicated desk

You get a fixed spot in the workspace — no packing up at the end of the day, no hunting for a free seat when you arrive. It's a comfortable middle ground: more affordable and flexible than a private cabin, but still a space that's consistently yours, alongside other founders and teams working out of 43 Workspace.
